01 / How it works

Know what to train. Every single day.

  1. 01

    Take the 5-minute level test

    A few max sets and a plank on the clock. Upside places you on the right step of every ladder, so you never start too easy or too hard.

  2. 02

    Train the session it builds for you

    Two to four times a week, 20 to 45 minutes. Each exercise comes with three cues, the two most common mistakes and a rest timer.

  3. 03

    Pass a test set, unlock the next step

    No guessing. Hit the target in a test set and you climb a rung. Too heavy today? Take it easier, without losing anything.

05 / A coach, not a drill sergeant

Built for people who don't feel strong enough yet.

Honest level-ups

You move up through a test set with a clear target, plus a quick form check. Never on a feeling.

Technique before volume

Three cues and the two most common mistakes for every step, with a moving figure that shows you how.

Take it easier

Something hurts, or two sets felt maxed out? Upside steps you back, without shame and without losing your progress.

Works in the park

Everything lives on your phone. No account, no signal needed. Just you, a bar and a bit of grass.
